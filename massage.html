Questions

Massage therapy FAQ

What kind of massage do you offer?

Deep tissue, performance and recovery-focused massage, not a relaxation spa massage. Built to work out tightness, improve mobility, and address nagging aches from training or everyday activity.

How long are sessions and what do they cost?

Sessions typically run 30 to 90 minutes and can be booked through our scheduling app. Pricing generally ranges from about $75 to $175, depending on the therapist and session length.

Is massage right for me if I have an injury?

Massage is best for general maintenance, mild tightness, or everyday discomfort. For a more significant or unresolved injury, we may recommend completing an evaluation with Dr. Nisar instead.

What should I bring to my massage session?

We recommend wearing something easy to work around, and bringing a change of clothes if you plan to use the cold plunge or sauna afterward.
