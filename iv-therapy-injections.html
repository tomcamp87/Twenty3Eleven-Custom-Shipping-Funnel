Questions

Injection FAQ

What injections do you offer, and what do they cost?

We offer a range of IM injections. You can view our full menu for specifics, and pricing generally runs from about $25 to $75 per injection depending on type.

Do I need clearance before my first injection?

Yes. First-time clients complete a consent form and a clearance exam with our nurse practitioner, in person or via telehealth (the Good Faith Exam). Vital signs are checked, and you wait 5 to 10 minutes after your injection so our medical staff can confirm there is no negative reaction.

Is there a fee for the clearance exam?

It is waived if you purchase a pack of injections as a first-time client. A clearance fee applies for a single injection.

Who administers the injection?

A registered nurse or nurse practitioner, hired and supervised under our medical director's protocols, after clearance is complete.

Is this covered by insurance?

No. Asylum operates on a 100% cash-pay basis; insurance is not accepted for any service.

How often should I get an injection?

This varies by injection type and by client. We typically recommend starting at least once a month, with some clients coming in as often as weekly, depending on your goals, health status, and our team's assessment.

Are there side effects?

Most people tolerate injections well. Mild, temporary effects such as soreness, redness, or slight discomfort at the injection site can occur and typically resolve within a day or two.

How soon will I feel the effects?

This varies from person to person. We do not make guarantees or claims about specific timelines or outcomes.
