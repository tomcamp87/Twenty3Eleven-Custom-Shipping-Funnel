The Asylum system

Personalized coaching, delivered in person and built for real life

1. Understand your starting point

We discuss your goals, training history, current habits, schedule, preferences, and obstacles.

2. Build your plan

Your coach creates individualized nutrition, training, and supplementation guidance based on your goals and current situation.

3. Coach you through execution

Coaching is delivered in person, with ongoing contact from your coach. The exact combination depends on your location, needs, and coaching plan.

4. Review and adjust

Regular check-ins help identify what is working, what is difficult, and where your plan needs to change.

5. Access the wider Asylum environment

IV therapy, lab testing, supplements, chiropractic, massage, acupuncture, sauna, cold plunge, and other services are available separately for clients who choose to use them.

Any labs are reviewed by the appropriate clinical team. Coaching is informed by your data and goals, but your coach does not diagnose, treat, order, or interpret labs.

Questions

Coaching FAQ

What is health & wellness coaching?

Coaching is where the week-to-week execution happens, a coach rather than a PDF. A plan that adjusts to your real life, with ongoing accountability and support between visits.

What does coaching cost?

Coaching starts at $250 per 4-week cycle. Longer commitments come with a reduced per-cycle rate, ask our team about current options.

Do I need to commit long-term?

No. Pay-as-you-go is available at $250 per 4-week cycle. Longer commitments lower the per-cycle rate.

What is included in a coaching cycle?

One-on-one access to a personal coach you can reach with questions day to day, plus a nutrition, training, and supplementation plan built around your current status and your goals. Your plan is adjusted week to week based on your check-ins.

Is coaching a guaranteed weight-loss or transformation program?

No. Coaching is individualized support and accountability. Results vary by person, and we do not guarantee specific outcomes or timelines.

What is your cancellation or pause policy?

This is handled case by case. When you sign on for a commitment term, you are locked in for that term. As you approach the end of your commitment, our team will discuss extending or adjusting your plan with you.
