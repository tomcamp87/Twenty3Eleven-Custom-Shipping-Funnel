Common questions

Is Asylum's service covered by insurance?

No. Asylum operates on a 100% cash-pay basis across every service: IV therapy, injections, lab testing, coaching, sports performance and recovery, and supplements.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is done through links throughout our site. The right booking link is included in each service section, so you can book directly from the page for the service you are interested in.

What paperwork or forms should I expect as a new client?

New clients can expect to complete basic consent forms, and depending on the service, you may also need a brief clearance exam with one of our medical professionals before treatment.
