HEADER VIDEO
------------
header.mp4 is the looping background used in every page hero, shown behind a dark
gradient overlay. A subtle, auto-generated placeholder (slow pan over a clinic shot)
ships here so the headers work out of the box.

To use the real footage: export the client's "Asylum Idea.mp4" as an H.264 .mp4
(1920x1080 or larger, silent, ideally under ~8 MB and 10-20s for fast loading) and
replace this file, keeping the name header.mp4. No HTML changes needed.
